## Deliverable A: Handbook Coverage and Structure

### 1. Document & Regulatory Architecture

The *AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH) 2024–25 to 2026–27* is a legal regulatory instrument notified pursuant to the All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987 (Act 52 of 1987). The structural contents across its 230 pages are organized as follows:

```
├── Preamble & Statutory Mandates (AICTE Act 1987 §§ 10, 11)
├── Chapter I: Grant of Approval for New Technical Institutions
├── Chapter II: Grant of Extension of Approval (EoA) for Existing Institutions
│   └── Sub-categories: Intake variation, new levels, mergers, conversions, closures, locations
├── Chapter III: Collaboration & Twinning Programmes (Indian & Foreign HEIs)
├── Chapter IV: Grant of Approval for Universities (Central, State, Deemed, Private)
├── Chapter V: Open and Distance Learning (ODL) and Online Learning (OL)
├── Chapter VI: Standard Norms and Requirements (Section A: General; Section B: Essential/Desirable)
├── Chapter VII: Penal Action on Violation of Norms
├── Chapter VIII: Do's and Don'ts & Administrative Clarifications
├── Annexures 1 – 19:
│   ├── Annexure 1 & 2: Document submission checklists (New vs. Existing)
│   ├── Annexure 3: Norms for Land, FSI/FAR, and Built-up Areas (Instructional, Admin, Amenities, Circulation)
│   ├── Annexure 4: Computing, Software, Internet Bandwidth, Library, and Laboratory Norms
│   ├── Annexure 5: Faculty Requirements, Cadre Ratios (1:2:6), and Teacher-Student Ratios
│   ├── Annexure 6: Norms for Standalone PGDM / PGCM Institutions
│   ├── Annexure 7: Scrutiny and EVC Process Workflows
│   ├── Annexure 8: Entry Qualifications, Durations, and National Credit Framework (NCrF) Levels
│   ├── Annexure 9 – 13: Specialized Curricula, Working Professionals, Nomenclature Matrix
│   └── Annexure 14 – 19: Competent Authorities, Board Composition, Mandatory Disclosures
├── Affidavits (Formats 1 – 12): Sworn legal undertakings (Stamp Paper / E-Stamp with DSC)
└── Formats & Certificates (Formats 1 – 6; Certificates 1 – 3): Architect, Structural, Bank verification

```

### 2. Comprehensive Coverage Checklist & Verification Register

* **Total Document Pages Reviewed:** Pages 1 through 230 (Inclusive of all chapters, regulatory clauses, comparative tables, footnotes, procedural charts, affidavits, and engineering certificates).
* **Unreadable Pages / Extraction Gaps:** No fatal corruptions. Architectural line diagrams and flowcharts (e.g., Annexure 7, page 100) represent cyclical administrative decision loops (Scrutiny $\rightarrow$ Re-Scrutiny $\rightarrow$ EVC $\rightarrow$ SAC/SHC $\rightarrow$ Executive Committee/Council), which have been cross-verified with explicit textual descriptions in Chapter I (Clauses 1.8, 1.9, 1.10, 1.11) and Chapter II (Clause 2.23, Table 2.3).


* **Key Statutory Nuances:**
* *Three-Year Multi-Year Approvals:* Available to top NIRF, QS, NBA ( $\ge 30\%$ eligible courses), NAAC (score $\ge 3.01$), Autonomous, or $\ge 80\%$ sustained enrolment institutions.


* *No Upper Intake Cap:* NEP 2020 alignment removes fixed ceilings for existing high-performing institutions, shifting validation purely to infrastructural sufficiency and faculty cadre fulfillment checked via EVC.

### Process Life-Cycle Definitions & Boundary Safeguards

1. **Failure & Uncertainty Protection:** An unreadable document or ambiguous OCR result must never trigger an automated rejection. It transitions to `UNREADABLE_OR_AMBIGUOUS_EVIDENCE` and routes to a Scrutiny officer with highlighted image crops.
2. **External Source Downtime Handling:** When third-party verification portals (e.g., Council of Architecture, Sub-Registrar) time out, the system marks the check as `EXTERNAL_VERIFICATION_PENDING` without halting internal cross-checks.
3. **Traceability Mandate:** Every fact presented to an AICTE official must retain a direct JSON pointer to:
* Source PDF document filename.
* Absolute physical page number.
* Normalized extracted string and bounding box coordinates.

## Deliverable K: Coverage and Quality Audit

### 1. Dual-Sourced Provisions & Potential Ambiguities

* **Conflict between Chapter I (Table 1.3) and Chapter II (Intake Expansion):** Table 1.3 sets fixed maximum initial intakes for new institutions (e.g., 360 for UG Engineering). However, Chapter II, Clause 2.6 explicitly states that pursuant to NEP 2020, AICTE has *removed the upper limit on intake* for existing institutions. The system must cleanly branch: apply Table 1.3 ceilings strictly to `CAT-NEW`, but evaluate `CAT-INC` dynamically based on infrastructure and faculty readiness verified via EVC.


* **Jurisdiction in Course Renaming:** Chapter II, Clause 2.18 states renaming is permissible *only* if the university does not offer the handbook's exact nomenclature. Cross-checking requires ingest of specific state university affiliation catalogs.



### 2. Dependencies on Unspecified External Databases

* The handbook presumes external validity for Court Orders, National Anti-Ragging compliance records (`antiragging.in`), and Chartered Bank RTGS settlements.


* *Platform Safety Mandate:* If external verification APIs for land records or municipal occupancy certificates are offline or legally unavailable, the platform must assign status `EXTERNAL_VERIFICATION_PENDING` and present the original PDF to the Scrutiny Committee without generating a false-positive violation.
